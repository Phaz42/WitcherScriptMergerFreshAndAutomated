Your own "no conflicts" image
=============================

When there are no conflicts, SM-FAE shows an image in place of the conflict list
(Options: "Show Witcher when you have no conflicts").

To show your own image instead, put it in this folder with one of these names:

  No-Conflicts.png        (or .jpg, .jpeg, .gif)   - shown with both themes
  No-Conflicts-Dark.png   (or .jpg, .jpeg, .gif)   - shown with the dark theme
  No-Conflicts-Light.png  (or .jpg, .jpeg, .gif)   - shown with the light theme

A theme's own image takes precedence: with No-Conflicts.png and No-Conflicts-Dark.png both here,
the dark theme shows No-Conflicts-Dark.png and the light theme shows No-Conflicts.png.

- Animated GIFs play.
- Files larger than 20 MB, or that are not a readable image, are ignored.
- Without any of your own images for a theme, SM-FAE shows its default image.
- A changed image shows at the next refresh, or when SM-FAE is restarted.

Updates of SM-FAE never put anything in this folder except this file, so your images stay when you update.
